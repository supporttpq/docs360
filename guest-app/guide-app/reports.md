# Reports

#### Overview

The **Reports** section is where a guide completes and submits a report from the Guide App. It is one of the ten sections on the Guide App **Home** screen. Guide masters receive the reports that guides submit. A guide master logs in to the Guide App, opens **Reports** and loads them.

The **Reports** screen lists three report types:

* **Excursions** — a form with fields about an excursion, such as trip evaluation, food, water and bus general state.
* **Hotels** — a form with fields about a hotel, such as cleaning, restaurant food, defects and compensation.
* **GRS** — a form with fields about a booking, such as problem, action, solution and compensation

#### Purpose

Use **Reports** to:

* Record feedback on an excursion with the **Excursions** form.
* Record the condition of a hotel and any guest problems with the **Hotels** form.
* Send the completed report from the app, with your name already filled in the **Guide** field
* As a guide master, review the submitted reports, filtered by date, report type and guide..

#### Preconditions

* You can log in to the Guide App with your Tourpaq username and password. See [Guide App](../../destination-mobile-guide-app.md).

#### How-to

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Open Reports**

1. Log in to the Guide App.
2. On the **Home** screen, tap **Reports**. You can also open the side menu with the burger icon at the top left and choose **Reports**.

The **Reports** screen lists **Excursions**, **Hotels** and **GRS**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (20).jpg" alt="Reports screen of the Guide App listing three report types: Excursions, Hotels and GRS" width="375"><figcaption><p>The Reports screen, opened from the Guide App Home screen.</p></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Fill in an Excursion report**

1. On the **Reports** screen, tap **Excursions**.
2. Check that **Guide** shows your name. The app fills it in.
3. Tap the calendar icon next to **Start Date** and **End Date** to pick the dates.
4. Complete the remaining fields, then scroll to the end of the form. See the Field Reference below.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (21).jpg" alt="Empty Excursion report form in the Guide App with the Start Date, End Date, Excursion, Guide, Guest Number and Trip Evaluation fields; the Guide field shows the logged-in guide" width="375"><figcaption><p>The Excursion form with its date, rating and bus fields.</p></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Submit a Hotel report**

1. On the **Reports** screen, tap **Hotels**.
2. Check that **Guide** shows your name. The app fills it in.
3. Complete the fields, then scroll to the end of the form.
4. Tap **SUBMIT**.
5. When the app asks **Do you want to send this report?**, tap **YES** to send it, or **NO** to cancel.
6. When the app shows **The report was sent!**, tap **OK**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (22).jpg" alt="Completed Hotel report form in the Guide App, showing the Guide name, Reception Service, Cleaning, Restaurant Food, Defects and Unhappy Guests fields and the SUBMIT button" width="375"><figcaption><p>The Hotel form, scrolled to the end, with the SUBMIT button.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (23).jpg" alt="Guide App confirmation dialog asking &#x22;Do you want to send this report?&#x22; with YES and NO buttons, over the completed Hotel form" width="375"><figcaption><p>The confirmation message shown after you tap SUBMIT on the Hotel form.</p></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

After you confirm with **YES**, the app shows **The report was sent!**.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
**Load submitted reports (guide masters)**

1. Log in to the Guide App as a guide master.
2. Tap **Reports**. The screen shows **From Date**, **To Date**, **Type**, **Selected Guide** and **LOAD**.
3. Tap the calendar icons to set **From Date** and **To Date**.
4. In **Type**, choose **All reports**, **GRS**, **Excursion** or **Hotel**.
5. In **Selected Guide**, choose **All guides**, or choose one guide. Each guide is listed as a name and a destination.
6. Tap **LOAD**.

Under **REPORTS**, the app lists each matching report as **Created on:** followed by the date and time.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (24).jpg" alt="Reports screen for guide masters with From Date, To Date, Type and Selected Guide filters and a LOAD button" width="375"><figcaption><p>The Reports screen where a guide master sets the filters and taps LOAD.</p></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (25).jpg" alt="Reports screen after tapping LOAD, with the filters set to All reports and All guides and one report listed under REPORTS as Created on 13 Oct 2026 at 14:59" width="375"><figcaption><p>The list of loaded reports. Each row shows when the report was created.</p></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
**Open a submitted report**

1. In the **REPORTS** list, tap a report.
2. Read the report. The app shows **Created on**, **Type**, and the fields the guide filled in.
3. Tap the back arrow at the top left to return to the list.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).jpg" alt="Excursion report opened from the list, showing Created on, Type Excursion and the submitted fields Excursion, Guide, Guest Number, Trip Evaluation, Local Guide, Schedule, Food, Water, Bus General State and Bus Microphone" width="375"><figcaption><p>A submitted Excursion report as a guide master sees it.</p></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

#### Field Reference

**Reports screen**

| Field          | Description                      |
| -------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Excursions** | Opens the Excursion report form. |
| **Hotels**     | Opens the Hotel report form.     |
| **GRS**        | Opens the GRS report form.       |

**Excursion form**

| Field                               | Description                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Start Date**                      | Start date of the excursion, picked with the calendar icon. |
| **End Date**                        | End date of the excursion, picked with the calendar icon.   |
| **Excursion**                       | The excursion being reported.                               |
| **Guide**                           | The guide submitting the report.                            |
| **Guest Number**                    | Number of guests on the excursion.                          |
| **Trip Evaluation**                 | Evaluation of the trip.                                     |
| **Local Guide**                     | Evaluation of the local guide.                              |
| **Schedule**                        | Evaluation of the excursion schedule.                       |
| **Program Description Consistency** | Whether the excursion matched its program description.      |
| **Food**                            | Evaluation of the food on the excursion.                    |
| **Water**                           | Evaluation of the water on the excursion.                   |
| **Uphappy Guests**                  | Unhappy guests on the excursion.                            |
| **Bus General State**               | General state of the bus.                                   |
| **Bus Microphone**                  | State of the bus microphone.                                |

**Hotel form**

| Field                   | Description                              |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Guide**               | The guide submitting the report.         |
| **Reception Service**   | Feedback on the hotel reception service. |
| **Cleaning**            | Feedback on hotel cleaning.              |
| **Restaurant Food**     | Feedback on the hotel restaurant food.   |
| **Defects**             | Whether there are defects at the hotel.  |
| **Unhappy Guests**      | Unhappy guests at the hotel.             |
| **Compensation**        | Compensation connected to the report.    |
| **Illness or Accident** | Whether an illness or accident occurred. |
| **Lost Property**       | Lost property at the hotel.              |
| **Hotel Trade**         | Hotel trade.                             |
| **Other Comments**      | Free comments about the hotel.           |
| **SUBMIT**              | Sends the report after you confirm.      |

#### **Load reports screen**

| Field              | Description                                                                               | Notes                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **From Date**      | First date of the period to load reports for. Tap the calendar icon to change it.         | Shown in the format `30 Sep 2026`. Which report date the period applies to is not verified.                                              |
| **To Date**        | Last date of the period to load reports for. Tap the calendar icon to change it.          | Shown in the format `14 Oct 2026`.                                                                                                       |
| **Type**           | Limits the list to one report type.                                                       | Options: `All reports`, `GRS`, `Excursion` and `Hotel`. The screen opens with `All reports`.                                             |
| **Selected Guide** | Limits the list to the reports of one guide.                                              | Options: `All guides`, then one entry per guide, written as the guide name and the destination. Which guides are listed is not verified. |
| **LOAD**           | Loads the reports that match **From Date**, **To Date**, **Type** and **Selected Guide**. | The matching reports appear under **REPORTS**.                                                                                           |

**Reports list and report view**

| Field                        | Description                                                           |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Created on** (list row)    | One submitted report, identified by the date and time it was created. |
| **Created on** (report view) | Date and time the report was created.                                 |
| **Type**                     | The report type, for example `Excursion`.                             |
| **Report fields**            | The values the guide entered in the form.                             |
