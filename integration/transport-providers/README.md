# Transport Providers

## Transport Providers

### Overview

The Transport Providers module in Tourpaq is used to manage external transport suppliers that provide services such as flights, buses, trains, ferries, and transfers.

Transport Providers define how transport-related data (including passenger lists, schedules, and operational messages) is communicated between Tourpaq and external partners. These providers are a key part of the transport distribution and reporting framework.

Through Transport Providers, Tourpaq ensures that all transport-related operations are synchronized, automated, and traceable across suppliers and internal booking workflows.

***

## Purpose

The Transport Providers module is designed to:

* Manage external transport suppliers in a centralized way.
* Define communication rules for passenger and transport data exchange.
* Automate transmission of passenger name lists (PNL).
* Support multiple reporting formats per supplier.
* Ensure accurate synchronization of transport operations.
* Reduce manual communication with carriers.

## Business Benefits

### For Tour Operators

* Automated communication with transport suppliers.
* Reduced operational workload for flight and bus reporting.
* Centralized supplier configuration.
* Improved accuracy of passenger data delivery.
* Flexible reporting per transport or supplier.

### For Operations Teams

* Clear visibility of transport communication rules.
* Ability to resend or adjust reporting schedules.
* Better control over supplier-specific requirements.
* Standardized handling of transport data across brands.

***

## Administration

### Centralized Control

Transport Providers are managed centrally within Tourpaq System Setup.

Benefits include:

* Consistent supplier configuration
* Easier maintenance of reporting rules
* Reduced duplication of supplier entries

### Monitoring

Administrators can monitor:

* Sent communications
* Scheduled reporting rules
* Supplier-specific data flows
* Error logs (via system services)
