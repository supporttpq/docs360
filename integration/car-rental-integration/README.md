# Car Rental Integration

The Car Rental Integration in Tourpaq enables travel agencies and tour operators to offer rental cars as part of the booking process. The integration is powered through **Auto Europe** and is handled within the **Extras** framework in Tourpaq, allowing car rental services to be sold alongside flights, hotels, transfers, and other travel products.

This integration centralizes car rental availability and pricing while maintaining a single booking workflow for agents and customers.

***

## Purpose

The Car Rental Integration is designed to:

* Allow rental cars to be booked together with travel packages.
* Retrieve car rental availability and pricing from Auto Europe.
* Reduce manual handling of external car rental bookings.
* Centralize supplier credentials and management.
* Support dynamic packaging scenarios where flights, hotels, and car rentals are combined into a single booking.
