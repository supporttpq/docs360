# Car Rental Integration

### Overview

The Car Rental Integration module in Tourpaq enables travel agencies and tour operators to search, quote, and book rental vehicles directly from external car rental providers as part of the booking workflow.

Car rental services are integrated into Tourpaq as dynamic products and can be sold as standalone services or combined with flights, hotels, transfers, and other travel components to create complete travel packages. The integration eliminates manual supplier communication by providing real-time availability, pricing, and booking capabilities directly within the platform.

***

## Purpose

The Car Rental Integration framework is designed to:

* Connect Tourpaq with external car rental suppliers
* Provide real-time vehicle availability and pricing
* Support dynamic packaging with other travel products
* Automate reservation creation and confirmation
* Centralize booking management within Tourpaq
* Reduce manual administration and supplier interactions
* Improve customer and agent booking experiences

***

## Supported Provider

Currently, the documented car rental integration available in Tourpaq is:

| Provider    | Purpose                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Auto Europe | Provides access to global car rental inventory, availability, pricing, and reservation services directly through Tourpaq. |

The Car Rental Integration module extends Tourpaq's dynamic travel ecosystem by connecting directly to Auto Europe, allowing agencies to search, price, and book rental vehicles without leaving the platform. Through real-time supplier connectivity and seamless integration with flights, hotels, and other travel products, Tourpaq enables travel businesses to deliver complete travel packages from a single booking environment.
