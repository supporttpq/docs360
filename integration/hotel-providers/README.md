# Hotel Providers

## Overview

The Hotel Providers module enables Tourpaq to connect with external hotel suppliers, bed banks, and accommodation distribution platforms. Through these integrations, Tourpaq can automatically retrieve hotel availability, room types, pricing, facilities, images, and other accommodation data directly from external providers.

Hotel Providers play a central role in Tourpaq's dynamic packaging and accommodation management capabilities, allowing travel companies to combine their own hotel contracts with dynamically sourced inventory from external suppliers.

***

## Purpose

The Hotel Providers module is designed to:

* Automate hotel data imports from external suppliers.
* Maintain up-to-date availability and pricing.
* Reduce manual hotel creation and maintenance.
* Import hotel descriptions, facilities, and images.
* Support dynamic accommodation sourcing.
* Enable centralized management of provider credentials.
* Support dynamic package creation using external accommodation inventory.

***

## Supported Providers

Tourpaq currently supports integrations with multiple accommodation providers and hotel distribution systems.

| Provider                   | Purpose                                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hotelbeds                  | Hotel inventory, contracts, availability, pricing, facilities, and images |
| D-EDGE (formerly Availpro) | Hotel connectivity, availability, and pricing synchronization             |
| SkiStar                    | Accommodation and resort-specific inventory management                    |

Additional integrations may be available depending on customer agreements and system configuration.

***

## How Hotel Providers Work

The Hotel Providers framework acts as a bridge between external accommodation systems and Tourpaq.

```
Hotel Provider
      ↓
Availability & Pricing
      ↓
Tourpaq
      ↓
Hotel Search
      ↓
Booking Creation
      ↓
Reservation Management
```

Provider data is imported into Tourpaq and becomes available throughout the booking process, both in back office operations and online booking flows.

***

## Available Data

Depending on the provider, the following information can be imported.

### Hotel Information

* Hotel name
* Address
* Description
* Star rating
* Contact details
* Resort information

### Facilities

* Hotel facilities
* Room facilities
* Board types
* Property amenities

### Media

* Hotel images
* Room images

### Contract Information

* Room types
* Room configurations
* Occupancy rules
* Availability
* Prices

### Dynamic Data

* Availability updates
* Stop sales
* Inventory changes
* Pricing updates
