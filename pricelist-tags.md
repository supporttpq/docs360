# Price List Tags

## Overview

**Price list tags** let you label specific offers (price list lines / PLTA rows).

Use tags to filter offers and highlight selected offers on your website.

## Purpose

* Make selected offers easier to find.
* Enable custom filtering on the website.
* Highlight offers based on business needs (for example: “Special Deal”, “Early Booking”, “Family Offer”).

## Preconditions

* You must have access to the **Price List** menu.
* At least one price list line must already exist (transport + hotel + room).

## Instructions

### Step 1: Create a New Tag

1. Go to **Price List → Price List Tag**.
2. Click **New Tag**.
3. Enter a **Name** for the new tag.
4. Click **Save Tag**.

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/image (354).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Step 2: Apply a Tag to Offers

1. Go to **Price List**.
2. Filter to the offers you want (transport, hotel, room, dates, etc.).
3. Click **Display**.
4. Open **Column Filters**.
5. Enable **TAG CH** (and/or **TAG**) and click **Display** again.
6. From the displayed results, select one or more offers.
7. Set the **TAG** value to the tag you want.
8. Click **Save**.

The selected offers are now tagged. They can be filtered or highlighted on the website.
